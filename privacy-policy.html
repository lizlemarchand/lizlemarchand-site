Privacy Policy

This policy explains how E Lemarchand Services collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit lizlemarchand.com or sign up to receive emails. Your privacy matters and your data will never be sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who We Are

E Lemarchand Services operates lizlemarchand.com. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@lizlemarchand.com. For postal address requests, please reach out by email.

What Data We Collect

When you submit a form on this site (newsletter sign-up or archetype quiz), we collect your first name and email address. We do not collect payment information, sensitive personal data, or any data from minors under 16.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to send you emails you have opted in to receive, including our weekly newsletter and related content about sales confidence and business growth. We will never use your data for any other purpose without your explicit consent.

Who We Share Your Data With

Your name and email address are stored in Systeme.io (our email marketing platform) and processed via Zapier (our automation tool). Both are GDPR-compliant processors. No data is shared with any other third parties.

Cookies and Analytics

This website does not currently use cookies or analytics tracking tools.

Your Rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at contact@lizlemarchand.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The current version will always be available at lizlemarchand.com/privacy-policy.html.
